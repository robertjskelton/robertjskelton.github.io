![Desk](/images/desk/2.jpg)

1. [Uplift Desk (80x30) standing desk](https://www.upliftdesk.com/standing-desks/) - Huge desk with bamboo top, C-Frame with 2 grommet covers in the top. I also bought the Uplift cable management system that goes under the desk, and a magnetic cable hider that goes on one of the legs. Keeps the wires out of the way and hidden. 

![Desk](/images/desk/5.jpg)

2. [Uplift Moontower triple monitor arm](https://www.upliftdesk.com/moontower-monitor-arm-by-uplift-desk/?14036=18287) - keeps all your monitors off the desk for more room. 

3. [Herman Miller Aeron Chair](https://amzn.to/3QEh0iL) - bought used on FB Marketplace for $400, Size B fits me, Size A is for smaller people, Size C is for larger people.

4. [2x 24" monitors](https://amzn.to/3Qz9V2Y) + [1x 32" monitor](https://amzn.to/4fUwucG) - Each one of these monitors goes on the monitor stand, so none are sitting on the desk. I used to have 3x24" but one of them broke. This monitor stand can hold up to 3x27" monitors. You need monitors with VESA mounts (4 screw holes in a square pattern) on the back though, which is almost all monitors made now. 

5. [48x24" mouse pad](https://amzn.to/4eRDUMN) - huge mouse pad because it's a huge desk.

6. [Laptop tray](https://amzn.to/4vvGIW0) - I have one on each side of my desk, but only use one side

7. [USB-C Dock](https://amzn.to/4aWlQi3) - everything from my laptop goes into this

8. [Mouse - Roccat Kone Pro Air](https://amzn.to/4gzIJeW) - I liked that it can be wireless or wired and has customizable button lights. 

9. [Keyboard - Mistel](https://mistelkeyboard.com/products/aff922daf90083d4382a8e6583885571) - I liked the colors and wanted a full NumPad and mechanical keyboard.

10. [A few low profile surge protectors with flat tops](https://amzn.to/3QCH8dR) - helps them stay closer to the wall, something like this but I just bought some cheaper ones at Walmart.

![Desk](/images/desk/6.jpg)

11. [Cable wrap](https://amzn.to/3Qe8QgX) - helps tie together multiple cables and hide them for a cleaner look, can be cut to size with scissors 

![Desk](/images/desk/7.jpg)

12. [Walking pad](https://amzn.to/3So52dx) - that I don't use enough 

![Desk](/images/desk/4.jpg)

13. [Jabra headset](https://amzn.to/4a9SeO8) - mine is from 2017 but is still awesome.
